Your phone needs to trust this PC once. Phones only share their motion sensors with secure (HTTPS) pages, and this PC uses its own certificate.

  1. Use Safari for this page.
  2. Tap Download certificate below, then Allow.
  3. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install.
  4. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on OpenSteer local CA.
  5. Come back here. It continues by itself, or tap Continue.
